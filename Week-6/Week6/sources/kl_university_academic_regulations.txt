KL UNIVERSITY — ACADEMIC REGULATIONS & EVALUATION MANUAL
Document Version: 2026.1
Authority: Academic Council & Office of the Dean Academics

SECTION 1: COURSE STRUCTURE & CREDIT FRAMEWORK
1.1 Each course in the undergraduate program carries a defined credit weight based on lecture (L), tutorial (T), and practical/skilling (P/S) contact hours.
1.2 One academic credit corresponds to 15 lecture hours or 30 practical/skilling lab hours per semester.
1.3 Students must register for a minimum of 18 credits and a maximum of 26 credits per regular semester.

SECTION 2: EVALUATION AND GRADING SCHEME
2.1 Continuous In-Semester Evaluation (CIE) contributes 60% of the total course marks.
2.2 Semester End Examination (SEE) contributes 40% of the total course marks.
2.3 The overall course performance is computed on a 100-point scale:
    Total Score = (CIE Marks out of 60) + (SEE Marks out of 40).
2.4 Letter Grades and Grade Points:
    - S (Outstanding): 90 - 100 marks (Grade Point: 10)
    - A (Excellent): 80 - 89 marks (Grade Point: 9)
    - B (Good): 70 - 79 marks (Grade Point: 8)
    - C (Fair): 60 - 69 marks (Grade Point: 7)
    - D (Satisfactory): 50 - 59 marks (Grade Point: 6)
    - E (Pass): 40 - 49 marks (Grade Point: 5)
    - F (Fail): Below 40 marks (Grade Point: 0)
2.5 To pass a course, a student must secure at least 40% in SEE (minimum 16 out of 40) and at least 40% overall (minimum 40 out of 100).

SECTION 3: ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENTS & CONDONATION
3.1 A student must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each registered theory, lab, and skilling course to be eligible to appear for the Semester End Examination.
3.2 Condonation for attendance shortage between 65% and 74% may be granted by the Dean Academics only on valid medical grounds or university-approved sports/cultural participation.
3.3 Medical condonation applications must be submitted within 7 calendar days of recovery along with a registered physician's medical certificate and hospital discharge summary.
3.4 Students with attendance below 65% are not eligible for condonation under any circumstances and shall be detained in the course, requiring re-registration in subsequent semesters.

SECTION 4: SKILLING SESSIONS & PRACTICAL LAB POLICIES
4.1 Weekly skilling lab exercises must be submitted and evaluated within 7 calendar days of the scheduled lab session.
4.2 Late submissions incur a mandatory 10% mark deduction per week of delay up to a maximum of 2 weeks. Submissions beyond 14 days will receive zero marks.
4.3 Lab records and codebase implementations must be maintained in the student's personal GitHub repository with verifiable commit timestamps.
